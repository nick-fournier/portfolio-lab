Make vs buy

Is running our own strategies worth it compared with simply buying a fund? Passive index funds, factor funds, active ETFs, closed-end funds, big active mutual funds and Berkshire Hathaway, measured the same way as our strategies.

Not yet a fair fight. The funds are real money after real fees. Our strategies are backtests with modeled trading costs, chosen with hindsight on this same period, paying no management fee and no tax. Monthly rebalancing realizes short-term gains taxed as income, while an index fund defers almost all of its tax. A small edge over a cheap index fund can vanish after tax. Closed-end funds are measured at their market price, which can drift away from the value of their holdings.
