Market context

The economic backdrop is the same for every stock, so it can't pick stocks by itself. Here it is measured two ways: which stock traits have paid off under which conditions, and how the market fared after similar conditions. None of this times the market; at most it suggests being "a bit more cautious" while the strategies keep running.

The caution dial

How the market (SPY) did over the following 3 and 12 months, depending on the condition at the time: average return, share of periods that ended positive, and the average worst drop along the way.

Our market data starts in 2016, so this covers one long bull market (about nine years, overlapping windows). Over this sample the textbook warnings pointed the wrong way: "expensive" markets were followed by the best returns. It can describe, not predict; a longer history is needed before it adjusts any portfolio.
