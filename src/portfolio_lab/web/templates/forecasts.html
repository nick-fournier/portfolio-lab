Ranking (IC)
Each month, rank all stocks by forecast and again by their actual return that month, then take the correlation of the two rankings (Spearman): +1 = same order, 0 = no relation, −1 = reversed. Averaged over months. Real stock signals typically score 0.02–0.05.
Years right
Years whose average IC was above zero.
Best vs worst tenth
Each month, the average return of the tenth of stocks with the highest forecasts minus the tenth with the lowest, compounded over a year, before costs.
Tested
Each year predicted by a model trained only on earlier years; all stocks, next-month return relative to that month's average.

Forecast vs what happened

Each month, stocks are sorted by forecast and split into 20 equal groups (the lowest 5% of forecasts, the next 5%, and so on). Each dot is one group: its average forecast (across) against its average actual return (up), with a 90% margin. If forecasts were exactly right, the dots would sit on the dotted line. They rise in the right order but more slowly than the line: the forecasts exaggerate the differences between stocks. The shaded band shows how widely single stocks in a group landed (it runs far off the chart): most of any one stock's return is noise.
