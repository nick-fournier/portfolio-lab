The forecaster: a linear model on health, momentum (different in bear and rebound markets), year-on-year changes and valuation, plus gradient-boosted trees trained on what it gets wrong, added at half strength. Target: each stock's next-month return relative to the month's average, all stocks; each year predicted from earlier years only. IC: how well the ranking matched what happened (0 = chance; 0.02–0.05 is typical of real signals). The ranking is reliable; predicted sizes are not (they swing year to year), so use the order, not the numbers.
